# FileFlock Privacy Policy

**Last Updated: October 6, 2026**

FileFlock respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how FileFlock handles information when you use the application.

## Information Collection

FileFlock does not collect, store, sell, or share personal information or user data with the developer or third parties.

Files selected by the user are used solely to provide FileFlock's file-transfer functionality. FileFlock does not upload or store copies of those files on servers operated by the developer.

## Analytics and Tracking

FileFlock does not use advertising, tracking technologies, or analytics services to track users.

## Data Retention and Deletion

Because FileFlock does not collect or store personal information on developer-operated servers, there is no personal information retained by the developer to delete.

Users remain in control of files stored on their devices and can delete those files using the normal features provided by their device.

## Third-Party Services

FileFlock does not sell or share user information with third parties.

## Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated if FileFlock's functionality or data practices change. Any changes will be posted on this page.
