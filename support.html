Frequently Asked Questions

How do I transfer files to my Mac?

Select your files, tap Share, choose AirDrop, and select your Mac.

Why can't I see my Mac in AirDrop?

Make sure Bluetooth and Wi-Fi are enabled on both devices. Check the AirDrop receiving settings on your Mac.

Why can't I access certain photos?

Check FileFlock's Photos permissions in iPhone Settings. Some files may need to download from iCloud first.

Does AirDrop delete original files?

No. Sharing files through AirDrop does not automatically delete the originals.
